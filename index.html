About Me

I am an Applied Data Scientist with a focus on business analytics, machine learning, data visualization, and Generative AI. My work combines technical problem solving with an emphasis on understanding the business question behind the data.

Through academic and applied projects, I have worked with real-world datasets to perform exploratory data analysis, develop predictive models, evaluate model performance, build dashboards, and communicate analytical findings through clear visualizations.

My machine learning experience includes regression, classification, ensemble methods, feature engineering, and model evaluation using metrics such as MAE, RMSE, and R².

I have also developed hands-on experience with Generative AI technologies, including OpenAI APIs, LLM-based applications, fine-tuning, LoRA, document information extraction, and MLflow-based experiment tracking and observability.

I approach AI development with an emphasis on responsible use, recognizing the importance of privacy, fairness, transparency, security, governance, and human oversight when applying AI to real-world problems.
